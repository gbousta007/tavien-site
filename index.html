About Tavien

Tavien builds private AI assistants for business owners, executives and teams.

Our goal is simple: help people understand their business faster, work more efficiently and make better use of the information they already have.

Most companies already have the answers they need. The problem is that those answers are scattered across ERP systems, accounting software, CRM platforms, documents, emails, calendars, databases and internal tools.

Tavien brings that information together through a secure intelligence layer. Instead of searching through multiple systems or waiting for reports, users can ask questions in plain language, retrieve approved information and automate repetitive work.

Security is built into the architecture. Tavien does not give AI unrestricted access to core business systems. A controlled layer sits between the assistant and company data, enforcing permissions, limiting access and requiring human approval for sensitive actions when needed.

Over time, Tavien becomes more than an assistant. It becomes an intelligent operating layer across the business — helping people find information, monitor what matters and get work done.
