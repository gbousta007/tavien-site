■ 5.0 Security

Your business data stays under your control.

Giving AI access to company information requires more than connecting an API. Your company decides what the assistant can access, who can access it and what actions it is allowed to perform.

Read-only modeSensitive systems can begin read-only.
Explicit approvalSending, publishing, deleting or changing data can require approval.
Role separationAccess can be separated by employee role.
LoggingImportant interactions and actions can be logged.

Give AI access to your business without giving AI control of your business.
